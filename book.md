## A Little Message Before You Begin

Happy birthday, my love.

I wish I could put into words just how much you mean to me, but if you know anything about me by now, you'll know that even when I write paragraphs upon paragraphs, I still feel like I haven't said enough.

So, for your birthday, I wanted to make you something that you could keep. Something you could come back to whenever you want to remember how much you're loved, how special you are, and how much you've changed my life without even realising it.

This book is called *15 Reasons Why* because the 15th was the day our story really began. A day that neither of us could have predicted, but one that I'll always be grateful for.

Inside these pages are our memories, my thoughts, the things I adore about you, the things I don't say enough, and the promises I hope to keep as we grow together.

I know this isn't the biggest gift in the world, and I know that words alone can never show you the full extent of what I feel. But every page has a little piece of me in it, and every word is here because of you.

I hope that when you read this, you smile. I hope you laugh at some parts, get embarrassed at others, and maybe even realise a few things you didn't know about how I see you.

Most of all, I hope you feel loved.

Happy birthday, princess. Thank you for being you, and thank you for becoming such a beautiful part of my life.

I love you, Rahma. More than these pages could ever explain.

**Always yours,  
Musa**

# Chapter 1
## The Accident That Changed Everything

They say the best moments in life are the ones you never see coming. Sometimes, the smallest and most insignificant things end up becoming the beginning of something you never imagined you'd have.

For us, it was a misclicked button on a phone screen.

Your mum called, something happened on Snapchat, and somehow, you ended up adding me by accident. At the time, neither of us could have known what that little mistake would eventually mean. It was just another ordinary moment in an ordinary day.

But looking back now, it's funny to think that something so small could lead to something so important.

When I first saw your pictures, I was genuinely captivated. You were beautiful in a way that made me want to know more about you. But a picture is only a picture. It can't show you the way someone laughs, the way they speak, the little expressions they make, or the feeling you get when you're actually standing in front of them.

And I was about to find all of that out.

The next day, on the 15th, I went to Barking Park to meet you after school. I remember how different everything felt when I finally saw you in person. I already thought you were beautiful, but seeing you right there, in front of me, was something else entirely.

You had this gentleness about you that made me feel comfortable almost immediately. There wasn't anything I needed to pretend to be or anything I needed to prove. Talking to you felt natural, like we'd skipped the awkward beginning and gone straight to the part where we already knew each other.

We started walking around the park, talking and getting to know each other. There were no uncomfortable silences or moments where I was desperately trying to think of something to say. We just clicked.

And somewhere along that walk, you held my hand.

It's such a simple thing when you think about it. Two people walking through a park, holding hands. But I remember how it felt because, for that moment, I wasn't thinking about anything else. I wasn't worrying about tomorrow or thinking about what I needed to do later. I was just there, with you.

Eventually, we reached that quiet little place that would become *our spot*.

We stood close, hugged, and held each other. You rested against me, and for a little while, the rest of the world didn't seem very important. Then you looked up at me, I looked at you, and somehow, neither of us needed to say much.

And then we kissed for the first time.

I don't think I could ever properly explain that moment without making it sound smaller than it was. It was sweet, it was exciting, and it was the beginning of something that has since become one of the most meaningful parts of my life.

The funny thing is, neither of us needed a grand plan to get there. No carefully arranged introduction, no dramatic story about how we met. Just an accident, a conversation, a park, and two people who happened to find each other.

Sometimes I think about how easily we could have never met at all. You could have never added me, I could have never spoken to you, and the 15th could have been just another date on the calendar.

But it wasn't.

And I'm grateful that it wasn't.

Because somewhere between that accidental add and that first kiss, you became someone I wanted to know, someone I wanted to see again, and eventually, someone I couldn't imagine my life without.

If someone asked me what the most important day of my life was, I don't know what I'd say years from now.

But I know the 15th will always mean something to me.

It was the day a stranger stopped being a stranger.

It was the day our story began.

And it was the day I met you.

# Chapter 2
## The Person I Became After You

To understand why you mean so much to me, I have to be honest about the person I was before you came along.

Before the 15th, I wasn't exactly the person I'm proudest of. I had a careless mindset, and there were things about the way I thought and acted that I needed to change. I didn't always understand the importance of treating people's feelings with the respect they deserved. Sometimes, I was too caught up in my own world to think properly about the effect I could have on someone else.

My days followed the same routine. I'd wake up, scroll through TikTok, play games, play football, go to the gym, and do it all again. There was nothing necessarily wrong with enjoying those things, but I wasn't really thinking about what I wanted to become as a person.

I was just going through the motions.

Even the way I spoke reflected that carelessness. I would swear too much and throw words like *wallah* and *wallahi* around without always giving them the seriousness they deserved.

Then you came into my life.

I won't sit here and pretend that I became a completely different person overnight, because becoming better doesn't work like that. But meeting you made me look at myself differently. It made me think about the kind of man I wanted to be, the way I wanted to treat people, and what it actually meant to care about someone.

I started caring more about my words because I wanted them to mean something. Instead of throwing out empty promises, I wanted to make little promises to you that I could genuinely stand by. Instead of thinking only about what made me happy in the moment, I started thinking about how my actions affected you.

You made me want to be more patient, more thoughtful, and more intentional with the person I was becoming.

And the thing is, you never needed to sit me down and give me some huge speech about changing. Sometimes, just knowing that someone as lovely as you had chosen to let me into their life was enough to make me want to do better.

You brought a kind of *noor* into my life that I hadn't really understood before. Things that once felt ordinary started feeling meaningful. My days had something to look forward to. My phone became a little more interesting. Even when I was doing something completely unrelated to you, there was always the thought that I'd get to talk to you later.

You made me realise that life could be about more than repeating the same day over and over.

And I want you to know something, my love. I don't want to become a better person only because I'm scared of losing you. I want to become better because meeting you helped me recognise the person I could be.

I want to be someone who treats you well, who respects you even when we're disagreeing, who listens when you're speaking, who admits when he's wrong, and who keeps trying even when being better takes effort.

You haven't magically fixed every flaw I have, and I don't expect you to. That's my responsibility. But you have given me a reason to look at those flaws honestly instead of pretending they don't exist.

And for that, I'll always be grateful.

If I can look back years from now and say that knowing you helped me become kinder, wiser, and more respectful, then meeting you will have changed my life in a way that goes far beyond romance.

You didn't just become someone I love.

You became someone who made me want to love better.

And that's something I'll carry with me forever.

# Chapter 3
## Your Heart, and Everything You've Been Through

One of the things I've come to understand about loving someone is that you don't only get to know the parts of them they show to the world.

You also begin to understand the things they find difficult to talk about, the experiences that have shaped them, and the feelings they sometimes struggle to explain.

As we grew closer, you started trusting me with parts of your past. You told me about things that had hurt you, about the difficulties within your family, and about some of the emotional struggles you'd carried long before I came into your life.

I remember how it made me feel to realise that someone who could bring so much happiness into my day had also experienced so much sadness.

It made me wish that life had been gentler with you.

I wished you hadn't had to experience certain things so young. I wished that the people who were supposed to make you feel safe had always given you the care and consistency you deserved. And I wished I could somehow take away the difficult memories that still followed you into the present.

But I've also come to understand that loving you doesn't mean pretending I can erase your past.

I can't go back in time and change what happened. I can't promise that you'll never have another difficult day, and I can't always find the perfect words to make everything feel better.

What I can do is listen.

I can take your feelings seriously instead of dismissing them. I can give you space when you need it and comfort when you want it. I can remind you that you don't have to pretend to be okay just because you don't want to worry anyone.

I can learn how to support you in the way that actually helps you, rather than assuming I always know what's best.

And I can remind you that the things you've experienced do not define every part of who you are.

Because when I look at you, I don't see a collection of painful memories. I see Rahma.

I see a girl with a beautiful smile, a funny personality, a caring heart, and so many little qualities that make her who she is. I see someone who deserves to experience happiness without feeling guilty about it, someone who deserves kindness without having to earn it, and someone who deserves to feel safe enough to be herself.

I know there may be days when you don't see yourself the way I see you. There may be times when the world feels heavier than usual, when you don't feel like talking, or when even the simplest things seem difficult.

On those days, I don't expect you to put on a smile just to make me feel better.

You can be honest with me. You can have a quiet day. You can tell me that you aren't okay.

Your feelings won't make you any less lovable.

And if something feels too heavy to carry alone, I hope you'll let the people you trust support you, including people who can help you beyond what I know how to do.

I love being someone you can talk to, but I also want you to have all the support and care you deserve.

My love, you don't need to be happy every second to be precious to me. You don't need to hide the difficult parts of yourself to keep my love. You don't have to earn affection by being cheerful, easy to understand, or perfectly okay.

You are allowed to be human.

And I hope that, little by little, life gives you more reasons to smile than it gives you reasons to worry.

I hope you learn to look at yourself with the same kindness you show other people. I hope you recognise how much you have to offer the world, even on days when you struggle to recognise it yourself.

Most of all, I hope you know that your past is part of your story, but it doesn't get to decide everything that comes next.

There are still so many beautiful things ahead of you, Rahma.

And I hope I get to watch you discover them.

# Chapter 4

Love is easy to describe when everything is going well.

People grow. Circumstances change. Sometimes we misunderstand each other, sometimes we have difficult days, and sometimes the future looks nothing like the picture we had in our heads.

But I do know what I want to bring into our relationship.

I want honesty. I want patience. I want us to be able to speak openly, even when the conversation isn't easy. I want us to understand that loving each other doesn't mean we'll always agree, but it does mean we should treat each other with respect when we don't.

I want to keep choosing you in the ordinary ways that actually matter.

Not only through paragraphs and promises, but through how I speak to you when I'm annoyed, how I listen when you're upset, how I apologise when I've hurt you, and how I make an effort even when life gets busy.

Because I don't want our love to exist only in beautiful words.

I want it to exist in the way we treat each other.

Sometimes I think about the idea of forever. People say it so easily, as though loving someone means knowing every detail of what will happen next. But I think forever is something you build through the choices you make, one day at a time.

It's the little decisions to be honest instead of hiding things, to communicate instead of assuming, and to remember that the person beside you has feelings just as real as your own.

And if life ever becomes difficult for us, I hope we remember how we started.

I hope we remember the accidental Snapchat add, the walk through Barking Park, the way we held hands, and the little spot that became ours.

I hope we remember that before all the plans and expectations, we were simply two people who found something special in each other.

My love, I can't promise that every day will be perfect. I can't promise that we'll never disagree or that we'll always know exactly what to do.

But I can promise that I'll take what we have seriously. I'll keep learning how to love you well, and I'll do my best to make sure my actions match the words I write.

And if we ever find ourselves in a difficult chapter, I hope we don't immediately assume that the story has to end.

I hope we talk. I hope we listen. I hope we give each other the chance to understand.

Because you're not just someone I want to make memories with. You're someone I want to grow alongside.

And whatever tomorrow brings, I'm grateful that today, I get to call you my love.
